If the user's initiating message is only `Start`, `Build`, `Go`, `Continue`, `/goal`, or equivalent, interpret it as:

> Execute Tessellate autonomously according to AGENTS.md and docs/BUILD_ORDER.md, starting from the first incomplete item and continuing until a documented stopping condition applies.

The only valid stopping conditions are those in `AGENTS.md`.
