## Field meanings

- **Status** records whether implementation is present or conditional; it does
  not mean the feature passed an end-to-end audit.
- **Tests** points to test coverage found during inventory. “No dedicated test
  found” means no test was found in this pass, not that the route has no shared
  code coverage.
- **Known defects** remains “audit pending” until the corresponding ordered
  hardening gate is completed. Explicit source-level candidates are listed in
  the notes below and remain open for runtime verification.
- **Capability tier** records the intended support boundary, not a passing
  quality result. Core nominations remain uncertified until their ordered
  hardening gates pass; dimensions are tracked in `quality/FEATURE_MATRIX.md`.
- **Persistence** lists the implementation's apparent state boundary. Actual
  migration, failure, quota and cross-session behaviour is unverified unless
  already stated by a specific passing test.
